Cochran Street

Insurance and Claims

Dealing With Insurance After a Simi Valley Accident

Insurance adjusters handling claims from Simi Valley apply consistent internal practices to this part of eastern Ventura County. You are generally not required to give a detailed recorded statement to an insurance company, including your own, before speaking with an attorney.

Because Simi Valley borders Los Angeles County and sees substantial cross-county commuter traffic, claims here can sometimes involve carriers and adjusters from outside the immediate area — another reason to have your coverage and the other driver's coverage reviewed early.

Right After an Accident

What to Do After an Accident in Simi Valley

Get Medical Treatment

Seek medical attention promptly, even if injuries seem minor at first. Prompt treatment protects your health and creates a medical record close in time to the accident.

Document the Scene

Photograph vehicle positions, visible injuries, and the exact intersection or freeway interchange involved, since this level of detail can matter later.

Be Cautious With Insurance Calls

Adjusters may request a recorded statement quickly, and what you say can later be used to dispute or reduce your claim.
