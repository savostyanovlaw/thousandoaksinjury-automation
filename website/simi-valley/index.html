After an Accident

Practical steps that can help protect a Simi Valley injury claim.

01

Get Appropriate Medical Care

Seek evaluation for injuries and follow reasonable treatment recommendations. Early records can help document when symptoms began.

02

Preserve Scene Evidence

Keep photographs, witness information, insurance documents, and details identifying the exact freeway interchange, street, intersection, or business involved.

03

Review Insurance Requests Carefully

The other driver’s insurer may request a recorded statement. Your own insurer may have separate cooperation obligations, so each request should be considered in context.

Insurance & Coverage

Freeway crashes and local-road collisions can present different evidence and coverage issues.

A 118 Freeway collision may involve multiple vehicles, changing lanes, substantial property damage, and several insurance policies. A surface-street crash may turn more heavily on signal timing, right-of-way, witnesses, and nearby video.

Uninsured or underinsured motorist coverage may also become important when the at-fault driver has inadequate insurance.

Simi Valley Personal Injury FAQ

Do Simi Valley cases get filed locally?

Some Simi Valley-area matters that proceed to litigation may be assigned within the Ventura County Superior Court system. The proper venue and courthouse depend on the particular facts and current court procedures.

What types of accidents do you handle in Simi Valley?

We handle car accidents, motorcycle accidents, pedestrian accidents, bicycle accidents, rideshare accidents, slip and fall incidents, and other personal injury matters.

Will I speak with the attorney directly?

Yes. Clients communicate directly with Alexey Savostyanov about their matters.

How much does it cost to hire a personal injury attorney?

The consultation is free. Personal injury matters are handled on a contingency fee set out in a written agreement, with case costs addressed separately in that agreement.

What if my accident happened on the 118 Freeway?

Freeway accidents can involve higher speeds, multiple vehicles, merging activity, and evidence that should be preserved promptly. We can review the specific facts during a free consultation.

What if the other driver did not have insurance?

If you carry uninsured or underinsured motorist coverage, you may be able to pursue compensation through your own policy. Coverage depends on the policy and facts.

Do you offer consultations in Russian?

Yes. Alexey Savostyanov is fluent in English and Russian.

How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in California?

In many California personal injury cases, the deadline is generally two years from the date of injury, while claims involving public entities can have much shorter notice deadlines. The correct deadline depends on the facts and parties involved.
