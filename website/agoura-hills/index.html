Insurance and Claims

Dealing With Insurance After a Agoura Hills Accident

Insurance adjusters handling claims from Agoura Hills may be assigned based on either Los Angeles County or Ventura County practices depending on where the accident occurred and how the claim is routed. You are generally not required to give a detailed recorded statement to an insurance company, including your own, before speaking with an attorney.

Because Agoura Hills sits directly on the county line, it's worth having an attorney confirm which jurisdiction and which insurance practices actually apply to your specific claim before you discuss settlement.

Right After an Accident

What to Do After an Accident in Agoura Hills

Get Medical Treatment

Seek medical attention promptly, even if injuries seem minor at first. Prompt treatment protects your health and creates a medical record close in time to the accident.

Document the Scene

Photograph vehicle positions, visible injuries, and the specific road or interchange involved — details that matter more in a border-area city like Agoura Hills.

Be Cautious With Insurance Calls

Adjusters may request a recorded statement quickly, and what you say can later be used to dispute or reduce your claim.
