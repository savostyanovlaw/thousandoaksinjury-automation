After an Accident

Practical steps that can help protect an Agoura Hills injury claim.

01

Get Appropriate Medical Care

Seek evaluation for injuries and follow reasonable treatment recommendations. Early records can help document when symptoms began.

02

Preserve Scene Evidence

Keep photographs, witness information, insurance correspondence, repair records, and any documentation identifying the exact road or interchange involved.

03

Review Insurance Requests Carefully

The other driver’s insurer may request a recorded statement. Your own insurer may have separate cooperation requirements, so the request should be evaluated in context before you respond.

Insurance & Venue

The county line can matter, but the facts of the specific accident control.

Agoura Hills is in Los Angeles County, immediately adjacent to Ventura County communities. Depending on where a collision occurred, where the parties reside, and other case-specific facts, litigation may proceed in a Los Angeles County court or another proper venue.

Insurance handling is separate from venue. The claim should be evaluated based on the policy language, available coverage, liability evidence, and damages rather than assumptions based only on county boundaries.

Agoura Hills Personal Injury FAQ

Does it matter that Agoura Hills is in Los Angeles County, not Ventura County?

It can. Depending on where your accident occurred and the specific facts, your case may be filed in Los Angeles County Superior Court or, in some circumstances, Ventura County. We can review this directly with you.

What types of accidents do you handle in Agoura Hills?

We handle car accidents, motorcycle accidents, pedestrian accidents, bicycle accidents, rideshare accidents, slip and fall incidents, and other personal injury matters.

Will I speak with the attorney directly, or with office staff first?

You speak with Alexey Savostyanov directly. There is no call center or intake department screening calls before they reach an attorney.

How much does it cost to hire a personal injury attorney?

The consultation is free. Personal injury matters are handled on a contingency fee set out in a written agreement, with case costs addressed separately in that agreement.

What if my accident happened on the 101 Freeway in Agoura Hills?

Freeway accidents often involve unique considerations such as speed, multiple vehicles, and merging activity near interchanges. We can review the specific facts during a free consultation.

What if the other driver did not have insurance?

If you carry uninsured or underinsured motorist coverage, you may be able to pursue compensation through your own policy. We can review your coverage and explain your options.

Do you offer consultations in Russian?

Yes. Alexey Savostyanov is fluent in English and Russian and can discuss your case directly with you in either language.

How long do I have to file a claim in California?

In many California personal injury cases, the deadline is generally two years from the date of injury, while claims involving public entities can have much shorter notice deadlines. The correct deadline depends on the facts and parties involved.
