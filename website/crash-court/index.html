Car A turns left on a green light. Car B goes straight through the same green. They collide. Who's at fault?

Voting opens when the crash finishes.

What California law says

Usually A. Under California Vehicle Code § 21801(a), a driver turning left must yield to oncoming vehicles that are close enough to be a hazard. A green light lets you enter the intersection; it does not give you the right of way over oncoming traffic.

But fault is not always 100%. California compares each driver's share of fault. If B was speeding or ran a light, B can share the blame, and A may still recover part of the damages. Every case depends on its facts.
