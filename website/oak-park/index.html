Right After an Accident

What to Do After an Accident in Oak Park

Get Medical Treatment

Seek medical attention promptly, even if injuries seem minor. Prompt treatment protects your health and creates a medical record close in time to the accident.

Document the Scene

Photograph the vehicles, visible injuries, and the exact intersection or street, since cross streets and landmarks can matter later. Collect contact information for any witnesses.

Be Careful With Insurance Communications

Insurance adjusters may request a statement quickly. What you say can later be used to dispute or reduce your claim.
