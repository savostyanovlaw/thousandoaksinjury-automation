Background

A legal career spanning two countries.

Alexey earned his first law degree from Kutafin Moscow State Law University (MSAL). For approximately fifteen years, he practiced law in Russia, developing extensive experience in litigation and legal counseling. His work included representing clients in court proceedings and advising businesses and individuals on a wide range of legal matters.

His professional background also includes teaching and authorship of various legal articles.

After relocating to the United States, Alexey continued his legal education at the University of Southern California Gould School of Law, where he earned his Master of Laws (LL.M.) degree. Following his move to the United States, he also worked with law firms in Southern California, gaining extensive experience in the U.S. legal environment and working on a wide range of civil matters.

After becoming licensed to practice law in California, Alexey continued representing clients in California civil matters and ultimately founded Savostyanov Law Corporation.

California Practice

Litigation experience brought to personal injury representation.

Alexey has substantial experience representing clients in matters before California state courts. His experience includes civil litigation, discovery, motion practice, negotiation, settlement proceedings, and other stages of litigation.

Through Savostyanov Law Corporation, Alexey represents people injured in motor-vehicle collisions and other incidents throughout California. The firm handles car accidents, motorcycle crashes, pedestrian injuries, Uber, Lyft and car-sharing accidents, delivery-driver injuries, dog bites, slip-and-fall and premises liability claims, serious injuries, and wrongful death claims.

As a Thousand Oaks personal injury lawyer, Alexey offers direct attorney communication from the firm’s nearby Westlake Village office. Clients throughout California can discuss their case with him in English or Russian.
