Dog Bite FAQ

Who may be responsible after a dog bite in California?

Responsibility depends on the facts, including ownership, location, conduct, and potentially applicable insurance. The incident should be investigated promptly.

What should I preserve after a dog attack?

Keep photographs, medical records, witness information, report information, damaged clothing, and communications concerning the dog or incident.
