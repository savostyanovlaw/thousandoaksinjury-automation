After an Accident

Practical steps that can help protect the claim.

01

Get Appropriate Medical Care

Seek evaluation for injuries and follow reasonable treatment recommendations. Early records can help document when symptoms began.

02

Preserve Evidence

Keep photographs, witness information, insurance correspondence, repair records, and medical documentation related to the incident.

03

Be Careful With Recorded Statements

The other driver’s insurer may ask for a recorded statement. Your own policy may impose separate cooperation duties, so review the request before responding.
