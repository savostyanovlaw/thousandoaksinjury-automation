Right After an Accident

What to Do After an Accident in Newbury Park

Get Medical Treatment

Seek medical attention promptly, even if injuries seem minor at first. Several medical providers serve the Newbury Park and greater Thousand Oaks area.

Document the Scene

Photograph vehicle positions, visible injuries, and the exact road or intersection — noting nearby landmarks like Borchard Road shopping centers can help later.

Be Careful With Insurance Communications

Adjusters may request a recorded statement quickly, and what you say can later be used to dispute or reduce your claim.
