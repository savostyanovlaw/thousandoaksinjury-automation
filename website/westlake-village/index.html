Right After an Accident

What to Do After an Accident in Westlake Village

Get Medical Treatment

Seek medical attention promptly, even if injuries seem minor at first. Urgent care and emergency facilities in the Conejo Valley area are accessible from most points in Westlake Village, and prompt treatment protects both your health and your claim.

Document the Scene

Photograph vehicle positions, visible injuries, and the specific intersection or stretch of road where the accident happened — landmarks like the Promenade or Westlake Lake can help fix the location later.

Be Cautious With Insurance Calls

Adjusters may request a recorded statement quickly. What you say can later be used to dispute or reduce your claim, so it's worth speaking with an attorney first.
