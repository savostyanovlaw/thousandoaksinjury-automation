After an Accident

Practical steps that can help protect a Westlake Village injury claim.

01

Get Appropriate Medical Care

Seek evaluation for injuries and follow reasonable treatment recommendations. Early records can help document when symptoms began.

02

Preserve Scene Evidence

Keep photographs, witness information, insurance documents, and details identifying the exact intersection, freeway location, parking area, or property involved.

03

Review Insurance Requests Carefully

The other driver’s insurer may request a recorded statement. Your own insurer may have separate cooperation obligations, so each request should be considered in context.

County-Line Considerations

Westlake Village’s location can make venue a fact-specific question.

Incidents around Westlake Village can occur on either side of the Ventura–Los Angeles County line. If litigation becomes necessary, the proper venue depends on where the incident occurred, where parties reside, and other legal factors.

Insurance coverage and claim handling are separate questions and should be evaluated from the policies, evidence, and damages.
