Uber and Lyft passenger injuries

Passengers can be injured when a rideshare driver causes a crash, another driver collides with the vehicle, or multiple parties share responsibility. Save the trip receipt, driver and vehicle details, photographs, witness information, and any communications with the platform. Seek medical attention when needed.

The available insurance may depend on whether the driver was offline, waiting for a ride request, on the way to a pickup, or transporting a passenger. Do not assume a particular coverage limit applies without reviewing the trip status, policy terms, and circumstances.

Injuries to other drivers, pedestrians, and cyclists

A collision involving a rideshare vehicle can harm people outside the ride, too. Liability and insurance can depend on the driver's conduct, another motorist's negligence, and the app status at the time. We can examine available evidence and possible responsible parties.

Turo and other car-sharing crashes are different

Turo and similar peer-to-peer vehicle-sharing arrangements are not the same as an Uber or Lyft passenger trip. The vehicle's owner, driver, booking terms, insurance or protection plan, and other applicable policies may affect the analysis. Preserve the reservation details and any incident report; do not assume rideshare rules apply.

Related claims and next steps

For general collision issues, see our car accident lawyer page. If a crash involves a delivery vehicle, see delivery-driver and delivery-vehicle injury claims. Promptly document injuries, repair estimates, available video, and insurance correspondence. Deadlines can be shorter when public entities are involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Uber or Lyft insurance always cover an accident?

Not automatically. Coverage depends on the driver's app status, the facts of the crash, applicable policies, and other available insurance.

What if the other driver caused the crash during my Uber ride?

A claim may involve the at-fault driver and other available coverage. The details require review of the collision and insurance information.

Is a Turo accident the same as an Uber or Lyft accident?

No. Turo involves a vehicle-sharing arrangement rather than a rideshare trip, and different contracts and insurance questions may apply.
